Can a five-minute break reset your focus?

The headline is irresistible. The evidence is a little more interesting.
JLJamie Lee · 4 min read
September 26 · Fictional demo article
🌿

A small study suggests short breaks may help people feel more focused. But “may help” is doing more work than the headline lets on.

What did they actually measure?

In this fictional example, 28 volunteers rated their own focus after a break. There was no randomized control group, and no objective test of memory or productivity.

The result is a useful question to investigate, not proof that five minutes will “reset” everyone's brain. Sleep, workload, and expectations could also affect the ratings.

A better question

What would a larger, controlled study find? A headline can start a conversation without being the final answer.
